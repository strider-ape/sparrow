# Changelog

## 0.2.0

### Looks

- **Eight looks**, each a separate skin in Poweramp's skin list and in the Sparrow app's new gallery. Picking a look
  works right away, even before Sparrow is verified:
  Sparrow (a deep color from the cover), Gradient, Aurora (the cover's color with glows in your wallpaper's tones),
  Blur, Vivid, Muted, Onyx (pure black for OLED screens) and Light.
- **Sparrow Light** fades to near white under the cover, so its dark controls stay readable.

### Player

- New **Background** option: a rich color picked from the cover (default), the blurred cover, vivid or muted.
- New default font, **Instrument Sans**, with Plus Jakarta Sans, Manrope, Inter and the system font as choices.
- **Progress bar styles:** Wave line (default), Line, Pill, Expressive and Glow, with previews in the options.
- **Volume slider** above the nav bar, with styles: Minimal (default), Line, Pill, Expressive and Glow.
- Larger title and artist, and less empty space between them and the progress bar.
- Large controls with a clean play/pause swap (no more overlapping icons).
- Phosphor icons in the nav bar (filled for the current tab) and for the Library categories.
- **Glass nav bar:** a floating pill on every screen, tinted by your wallpaper, with a glow behind the current tab.
- Accent color from your wallpaper (Android 12+) for the progress bar, volume and current tab.
- Dot-matrix font for the times and format line is now an option, off by default.
- The quick buttons row and the buttons on the cover are hidden by default; the track menu sits next to the title.
- Button backgrounds, Wave and Static seekbars, and every option combination were checked and fixed where needed.
  The Classic layout was removed, as it doesn't fit Sparrow's design.

### Lyrics

- New **Lyrics** option: Spotlight (default: big bold lines on the left, the current line bright and slightly larger),
  Centered zoom, Accent glow and Calm.
- Calm lyrics background in a deep color from the cover.
- Lower header with room before the first line, and clearly spaced lines.

### Notes

- Skin options take effect once Poweramp verifies Sparrow; until then Poweramp uses the defaults. Looks always work.

## 0.1.0

First public release: clean player layout, bare controls and a thin seekbar, dot-matrix times, a floating nav bar,
dark and light variants, and 8 skin options.
